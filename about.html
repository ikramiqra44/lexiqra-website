WHO WE ARE

About our company

LexIqra is a technology and innovation company driven by a passion for artificial intelligence, software development and problem-solving. We develop intelligent digital products and practical solutions intended to simplify complex tasks, support individuals and help businesses grow.

Our legal information assistant is one example of our approach: bringing useful tools together in an accessible mobile experience.

OUR MISSION

Make technology more accessible.

We aim to make legal technology more accessible by bringing artificial intelligence and practical legal tools together in one convenient platform, helping users explore legal questions, understand contracts and prepare document drafts.

OUR VISION

Build for the future.

We aspire to develop smarter features, expand our technology and introduce useful digital solutions that make complex processes easier to navigate.
